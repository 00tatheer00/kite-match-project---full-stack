What was done, area by area

All changes are live on www.kitepk.com. No customer data, prices or admin features were changed.

SEOGoogle search
  • Product titles now match how people search: “… Price in Pakistan | Order Online”
  • Descriptions show starting price and Cash on Delivery
  • Correct canonical URL for every product
  • Breadcrumb data on all public pages
  • Sitemap now includes product images and stable dates
SXOSpeed & experience
  • Banner video re-encoded: 2.1 MB on phones, 4.5 MB on desktop
  • Factory video: 1.6 MB preview; full film only on click
  • 45 images made 60–85% lighter, same size and look
  • Arabic/Urdu font self-hosted (no blocking request)
  • Banner image preloaded; no video→image “jump” on load
AIOAI-readable website
  • Homepage products now in the first HTML (visible to crawlers)
  • Product, Offer, shipping and return-policy data on every product
  • Duplicate “company” data removed: one Organization entity
  • Wrong logo & non-www links in old data fixed
GEOCited by AI assistants
  • /llms.txt now generated live with current prices & packages
  • ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini & Apple AI bots explicitly allowed
  • Contact points: customer service, sales & export
  • Private pages (/api, admin, checkout) blocked
AEODirect answers
  • New “Key facts & quick answers” section on every product
  • 7 questions each: price, sizes, how to order, COD, delivery, returns, maker
  • Answers built only from live catalog + Terms page (always accurate)
  • FAQ data so Google can show answers directly in results
CROMore orders
  • “Order on WhatsApp” button beside Add to Cart, product pre-filled
  • Trust boxes: COD · Delivery all over Pakistan · 48-hour replacement · Since 1974
  • Trust row + “no advance payment” under the Place Order button
  • Google Analytics: view_item, add_to_cart, begin_checkout, purchase, whatsapp_click

Google structured data

What Google & AI now read on a product page

Product & Offer

Name, images, brand “Kite”, maker, and every size with its PKR price, stock status, delivery charge and 48-hour return policy.

FAQ & Quick answers

7 short questions and answers, visible on the page and in Google’s data, so they can appear directly in search and AI answers.

Breadcrumb

Home › Products › Product. Google shows this path instead of a raw link.

One company identity

Mohsin Match Factory with address, founding year, contact points, social profiles and return policy, the same on every page.

Kept honest by design: no star ratings or reviews were added, because there are no real customer reviews yet. Fake ratings break Google’s rules and can remove the site from rich results.

How to verify

Check the results yourself in 10 minutes

1

Speed

Open pagespeed.web.dev, enter www.kitepk.com and look at the Mobile score.

2

Google rich results

Open search.google.com/test/rich-results with https://www.kitepk.com/products/kite-glow. Expect ✓ Product, ✓ Breadcrumbs, ✓ FAQ.

3

AI brief & crawler rules

Open www.kitepk.com/llms.txt (product prices list) and www.kitepk.com/robots.txt (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot listed).

4

Product page on a phone

Green “Order on WhatsApp” under Add to Cart, 4 trust boxes, and “Key facts & quick answers” at the bottom.

5

Sales tracking

Google Analytics › Reports › Realtime: open a product and add it to cart. You should see view_item and add_to_cart.

Recommended next steps

From here to 100%: work beyond the code

High · CRO, SEO

Real customer reviews

Collect reviews after delivery. They unlock ★ stars in Google and are the strongest trust signal for new buyers.

High · GEO, local SEO

Google Business Profile

Verify the Peshawar factory profile with photos, hours and the website link.

Medium · GEO

Mentions on other sites

Trade directories, news and supplier listings that link to kitepk.com. AI assistants rely on these.

Medium · SEO, AEO

Guides & blog

Helpful articles, e.g. “Best washing powder in Pakistan” or “How safety matches are made”.

After launch: in Google Search Console, re-submit sitemap.xml and use “Request indexing” on 2–3 product pages. Ranking changes usually appear within 2–6 weeks.

4. Shipping Charges Standardization (Resolved Rs. 0 Bug)

Elimination of Asynchronous Calculation Race Conditions
✓ Verified & Live

Identified and resolved the root cause behind shipping fees intermittently showing Rs. 0 during checkout due to asynchronous settings lookup.

Nationwide Shipping Policy Implementation:
• Packages 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6: Fixed Rs. 200 courier shipping (e.g. Kite Affordable for All: Rs. 400 + Rs. 200 = Rs. 600 COD).
• Bottom 3 Premium Bundles (#7, #8, #9): FREE Delivery (Glow Family Pack Rs. 2,250, Glow Bachat Mega Rs. 2,400, Vero Family Pack Rs. 2,950).
• Regular Products: Standard Rs. 200 delivery fee applies to all catalog items (even if priced above Rs. 2,000). No unauthorized free shipping is applied.
  • Zero-cost fallback guard removed; shipping correctly calculates based on explicit bundle eligibility.
  • Backend Order API independently recalculates and verifies subtotal and delivery fee before saving order to database.
  • Customer orders cannot be spoofed or manipulated on the client side.
